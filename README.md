A travel poster for a place that isn't there, drawn entirely in code.

**SOLMARA** is a cliffside town on a warm sea where two suns hang low
over the water at dusk, so every evening has two sunsets. Its landmark
is a tall spiral lighthouse on the highest cliff, its beam sweeping
across the water while sailboats come home under a violet sky.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.png (1024x1024, deterministic)
```

`main.py` is Python 3.13, stdlib + PIL only, no network. Running it
twice produces a byte-identical PNG.

## The poster

- Sky: deep indigo through violet to warm amber at the horizon
- Two suns — one large amber disc, one smaller pale-gold disc — with
  soft glows and light streaks on the water
- Dark silhouetted headlands, the left one carrying the spiral
  lighthouse with its glowing lamp room and sweeping beam
- A small village of lit windows on the cliff, three sailboats, a
  flock of birds, and the name SOLMARA set in large serif capitals
  with the tagline "WHERE THE SUN SETS TWICE"
